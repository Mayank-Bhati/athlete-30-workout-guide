MONDAY · PUSH

Chest + shoulders + triceps

60 min

Start the week with pressing strength, upper-chest fullness, shoulder width and four direct working sets for triceps.

Watch techniqueYouTube Short · incline press tutorial

01 / Upper chest

Incline machine press

2 sets6-10 reps90 sec rest

Set a moderate incline; press up and slightly inward.

Do not flare elbows straight sideways.

Other options: Smith incline press · Incline dumbbell press
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Jeff Nippard

02 / Chest

Flat Smith press

2 sets6-10 reps2 min rest

Plant your feet and lower the bar toward mid-chest with stacked wrists.

Do not bounce the bar or shorten the bottom range.

Other options: Flat dumbbell press · Flat machine press
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · cable fly tutorial

03 / Chest

Seated cable fly

2 sets10-15 reps75 sec rest

Bring your upper arms together in a controlled arc while keeping the chest lifted.

Do not force an excessive shoulder stretch.

Other options: Pec-deck fly · Single-arm cable fly
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Planet Fitness

04 / Front + side delts

Machine shoulder press

2 sets8-12 reps2 min rest

Keep ribs stacked and press without craning your neck.

Do not overarch the lower back.

Other options: Seated dumbbell press · Smith shoulder press
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Jeff Nippard

05 / Side delts

High-cable lateral raise

2 sets10-15 reps60 sec rest

Lead with your elbow and keep the shoulder away from your ear.

Do not shrug the weight upward.

Other options: Leaning cable lateral raise · Dumbbell lateral raise
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · cable Y-raise

06 / Side delts + lower traps

Cross-body cable Y-raise

2 sets12-15 reps60 sec rest

Raise into a Y with a light load and a long, neutral neck.

Do not turn it into a shrug.

Other options: Incline-bench Y-raise · Wall slide with lift-off
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Colossus Fitness

07 / Long-head triceps

Overhead cable extension

2 sets8-12 reps75 sec rest

Keep upper arms angled forward and extend completely.

Do not flare the elbows aggressively.

Other options: Rope overhead extension · Single-dumbbell extension
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Planet Fitness

08 / Triceps

Rope pressdown

2 sets10-15 reps60 sec rest

Pin elbows to your sides and fully straighten.

Do not let the shoulders roll forward.

Other options: Straight-bar pressdown · Single-arm pressdown

TUESDAY · PULL

Lats + mid-back + traps + biceps

70 min

Your V-taper and posture session, with three rowing grips, direct trap work and two biceps movements.

Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Davis Diley

01 / Lats

Neutral-grip lat pulldown

2 sets6-10 reps2 min rest

Keep your chest tall and drive elbows down toward your ribs.

Do not swing or pull behind your neck.

Other options: Medium overhand pulldown · Assisted pull-up
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · row grip guide

02 / Upper + mid-back

Wide-grip machine row

1 sets8-12 reps90 sec rest

Let the shoulder blades reach, then row toward the upper ribs.

Do not jut your head forward.

Other options: Chest-supported high row · Wide cable row
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · cable row guide

03 / Mid-back

Neutral-grip seated row

1 sets8-12 reps90 sec rest

Pause with the shoulder blades gently together and your neck long.

Do not turn it into a lower-back swing.

Other options: Close machine row · Chest-supported dumbbell row
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · row grip guide

04 / Lats + mid-back

Supinated machine row

1 sets8-12 reps90 sec rest

Keep elbows close and pull toward the lower ribs.

Do not curl the handle with your wrists.

Other options: Underhand cable row · One-arm supinated row
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · single-arm lat tutorial

05 / Lower lats

Single-arm cable lat pulldown

2 sets10-12 / side reps75 sec rest

Reach fully, then pull your elbow toward your back pocket.

Keep the shoulder away from your ear.

Other options: Half-kneeling one-arm pulldown · One-arm high cable row
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · pullover tutorial

06 / Lats

Bottom-half dumbbell pullover

2 sets10-15 reps75 sec rest

Use the stretched half of the motion while keeping ribs down.

Do not turn it into a triceps extension.

Other options: Cable pullover · Straight-arm cable pulldown
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · ATHLEAN-X & Huberman

07 / Rear delts + mid traps

Rope face pull

3 sets12-15 reps60 sec rest

Do one set from each cable height and finish with thumbs behind you.

Do not shrug or overextend your neck.

Other options: Low cable angle · Mid cable angle · High cable angle
Watch techniqueYouTube · Ryan Humiston

08 / Upper traps

Dumbbell shrug

2 sets8-12 reps90 sec rest

Lift your shoulders straight up, pause, then lower fully.

Never roll the shoulders in circles.

Other options: Smith shrug · Cable shrug
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · ATHLEAN-X

09 / Biceps

EZ-bar curl

2 sets6-10 reps75 sec rest

Keep your upper arms still and control the full lowering phase.

Do not lean backward to move the bar.

Other options: Cable curl · Alternating dumbbell curl
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · ATHLEAN-X & Huberman

10 / Biceps

Preacher curl

2 sets10-12 reps60-75 sec rest

Keep the upper arm supported and lower under control.

Do not hyperextend the elbow at the bottom.

Other options: Machine preacher curl · Spider curl

WEDNESDAY · LOWER A

Strength + posterior chain

65 min

Your heavier lower session: deep squatting, hip hinging and glute strength.

Watch techniqueYouTube Short · form tutorial

01 / Hamstrings

Seated leg curl

2 sets10-15 reps75 sec rest

Curl smoothly and squeeze without lifting the hips.

Do not let the stack crash down.

Other options: Lying leg curl · Single-leg curl
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Smith squat tutorial

02 / Quads + glutes

Smith squat

2 sets6-10 reps2-3 min rest

Use the deepest controlled range you can own; brace and drive through mid-foot.

Do not let your knees collapse inward or your pelvis tuck sharply.

Other options: Leg press · Smith box squat
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Squat University

03 / Hamstrings + glutes

Smith Romanian deadlift

2 sets6-10 reps2-3 min rest

Push your hips back with soft knees and keep the bar close.

Do not chase depth by rounding your back.

Other options: Dumbbell Romanian deadlift · Cable pull-through
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Mind Pump TV

04 / Glutes

Hip-thrust machine

2 sets8-12 reps2 min rest

Pause at full hip extension with ribs down and a slight pelvic tuck.

Do not finish by hyperextending your lower back.

Other options: Smith hip thrust · Dumbbell hip thrust
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · technique guide

05 / Quads

Leg extension

2 sets10-15 reps75 sec rest

Align the machine pivot with your knee and extend smoothly.

Do not kick explosively into lockout.

Other options: Single-leg extension · Slow-tempo leg extension
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Planet Fitness

06 / Inner thighs

Hip adductor machine

2 sets12-20 reps60 sec rest

Close the legs smoothly and control the return.

Do not force a painful stretch.

Other options: Cable standing adduction · Copenhagen plank
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · Jeff Nippard

07 / Side glutes

Hip abductor machine

2 sets15-20 reps60 sec rest

Open from the hips and pause; a slight forward lean may help you feel the glutes.

Do not bounce or lean far back.

Other options: Cable standing abduction · Lateral band walk
Watch techniqueYouTube Short · form tutorial

08 / Calves

Machine calf raise

2 sets10-15 reps75 sec rest

Pause in the stretch and rise onto the big toe.

Do not bounce through the bottom.

Other options: Leg-press calf raise · Smith standing calf raise

Neck-safe rule

Keep your head neutral, stop any movement that increases neck pain, and keep shrugs controlled. Radiating pain, tingling or weakness needs a qualified clinician.

Abs handled separately

No ab exercises are programmed here because you train them in your morning routine. Keep that work controlled and recoverable.

How to progress

Push, Pull, Lower A and Upper: finish set one near 2 RIR and set two near 1 RIR. Friday’s Lower B stays near 3 RIR. Add the smallest weight when both sets reach the top of the range cleanly.
