THE OTHER TWO DAYS

Recover like it is part of training.

SUN

Cricket only

Two T20 matches are your conditioning. Prioritize carbohydrates, fluids and electrolytes.

MON

Full rest

Easy walking and the posture reset are fine. No lifting and no hard running.

Neck-safe rule

Keep your head neutral, stop any movement that increases neck pain, and keep shrugs controlled. Radiating pain, tingling or weakness needs a qualified clinician.

Posture reset

Most days: chin tucks, wall slides, thoracic extension and a doorway chest stretch for 1-2 easy sets.

How to progress

Leave 1-2 good reps in reserve. When both sets reach the top of the range, add the smallest available weight next time.
